SESSION 2 • PARTNER GAME

🗣️ Say It Another Way

Explain the word without saying it. Your partner guesses.

How to play (with a partner)

🗣️
ExplainerHolds the phone and sees the word. Describes it without saying it.
🤔
GuesserCan't see the screen. Listens and shouts out guesses.
  1. A word appears with some red "don't say" words. The explainer can't say any of them.
  2. The guesser guesses out loud. When they say the word, the explainer taps ✓ They got it.
  3. Too hard? Tap Skip for a new word.
  4. Keep going as long as you like (or pick a timer). Tap End round to see your score, then swap roles.

Only tap ✓ when your partner really says the word. The game can't hear them, so it trusts you!
