SESSION 2 • THINKING GAME

🧐 Odd One Out

Three words belong together. One doesn't. Can you spot it?

How to play

  1. You see 4 cards. Each card has a word and a short meaning.
  2. Three come from the same family, for example all radar words.
  3. One comes from a different family (heat, LiDAR, hyperspectral...). Tap it!
  4. Then read what the three words have in common. It stays on screen until you tap Next.
EXAMPLE
🍎APPLE
🍌BANANA
🥕CARROT
🍇GRAPE

Apple, banana and grape are all fruits. Carrot is a vegetable, so carrot is the odd one out.

  1. 8 rounds. Right answer = 100 points, plus up to 50 bonus for being quick.
  2. The real learning is the "go together" part, so take a moment to read it.
